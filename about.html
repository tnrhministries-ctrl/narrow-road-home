Hey, I'm Daniel.

Husband, father, and the guy behind this channel — not because I had it all figured out, but because I felt God calling me to it.

Why I Started This

I know firsthand how trials can test our faith — and how God calls us to encourage one another in the middle of it.

God is close to the brokenhearted. I've felt that, and I wanted to build something that reminds other people of it too. This channel exists to encourage those who are struggling and to breathe new life into them — by sharing my own story, and by giving others a place to share theirs.

When you hear someone else's story, or a piece of yours in theirs, it changes something. It reminds you that you're not the only one who's felt overwhelmed, doubted, or wanted to give up. That's a lot of what you'll find here — my story, other people's stories, and the biblical stories of believers who were overwhelmed and came through. Not because the struggle wasn't real, but because God allowed it, grew them through it, and had their best in mind the whole time.

That's what The Narrow Road Home is about: real conversations about faith, doubt, and the fight to keep believing — for anyone struggling with life's challenges, whether that's your own battle, someone you love, or just the weight of the world right now.

A little more about me

  • Accepted Christ at age 4 in London, England
  • Attended Torchbearers Bible school in Estes Park, Colorado
  • Holds a degree in Organizational Leadership from Miami University (Ohio)
  • Has taught men's groups and led volunteer community groups
  • Started The Narrow Road Home to bring these conversations to more people

I'm also committed to bringing encouragement to believers who feel discouraged, isolated, or forgotten — and to anyone still curious or unsure about faith who's landed here. Wherever you are in this, you were born onto a battlefield, and you're not fighting it alone.

I believe God created you to keep fighting. Are you ready to stand firm?
